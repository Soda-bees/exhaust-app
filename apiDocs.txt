                ==UPDATE EXERCISE==
        URL         :admin/updateExercise.
        METHOD      :post.
        HEADERS     :{Authorization : Bearer your token}.
        BODY        :_id:document _id. 
                     exerciseData:{image , title , level , description}, 
                     objectId:optional if update an exersice inside workoutList. 
                     updatedWorkout:{name , tutorial , duration , workoutStatus , equipment}
        RES         :{ success: true, message: 'Exercise updated successfully.', updatedExercise }.
        COMMENT     :_id need exerciseData for update and objectId need updatedWorkout for update.

                ==DELETE EXERCISE==
        URL         :admin/deleteExercise.
        METHOD      :post.
        HEADERS     :{Authorization : Bearer your token}.
        BODY        :_id:document _id. 
                     objectId:optional if delete an exersice inside workoutList. 
        RES         :{ success: true, message: 'Exercise deleted successfully.', updatedExercise }.
        COMMENT     :_id required.If only have _id then delete whole document and have objectId then delete a single exercise inside workoutList.

        COMMENT     :_id and newWorkout object required.
